assets/car.bin.gz is baked by tools/bake_car from "Car Concept" (Khronos glTF Sample Assets,
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept).

It was made from a public domain (CC0) model by Unity Fan:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-concept-car-004-public-domain-cc0-4cba124633eb494eadc3bb0c4660ad7e

Changes made for Drift: the interior, wipers, brake pads, license plate, dark rim inserts and all textures
(including the Khronos logos) are left out; colors are the material base colors per vertex;
the geometry is converted to the game's axes, quantized and gzipped. The wheels fold flat in flight.
